## Question 1: Can you explain the approach more clearly at a conceptual level?

## 问题1：你能否从概念上更清楚地解释整个方法？

### Suggested viva answer — English

The central idea is to give RSSI and acceleration different roles. RSSI is the
spatial signal: it proposes where the participant is by selecting the room of
the beacon with the highest five-minute mean RSSI. Acceleration is the movement
signal: it does not predict a room, but tests whether rapid changes in the RSSI-
derived room are physically plausible. If the strongest-RSSI room changes while
movement is clearly present, the change may represent real movement. If room
labels repeatedly change during a long low-motion episode, I interpret this as
more likely to reflect beacon competition or signal propagation instability.
The final method therefore uses movement as contextual evidence to correct RSSI,
while retaining RSSI as the source of room identity.

### 中文理解

核心是让RSSI和ACC承担不同职责：

- RSSI是空间信号，用来提出“参与者可能在哪个房间”；
- ACC是运动信号，用来判断“RSSI显示的房间变化在物理上是否合理”；
- ACC不能单独决定房间；
- 房间名称仍然必须来自RSSI证据。

如果RSSI房间变化时ACC也显示明显运动，这可能是真实移动。如果参与者在一段长时间低运动
episode内，RSSI却在多个相邻房间之间反复切换，这更可能是beacon竞争、身体遮挡或无线传播
不稳定。因此，这是一种movement-supported RSSI correction，而不是用ACC代替RSSI。

### One-sentence version / 一句话版本

> RSSI proposes where the participant is; ACC tests whether changes in that
> proposal are physically plausible.

> RSSI负责提出位置，ACC负责判断这个位置变化是否符合真实运动。

## Question 2: What do ACC magnitude SD and mean RSSI mean intuitively?

## 问题2：ACC模长标准差和平均RSSI分别有什么直观含义？

### Suggested viva answer — English

I first combine the three acceleration axes into an orientation-independent
magnitude, (m(t)=\sqrt{a_x^2+a_y^2+a_z^2}). Within each five-minute window, the
standard deviation of this magnitude describes how much the acceleration signal
fluctuates around its local mean. A small value means the wrist signal is stable;
a larger value generally indicates movement or vibration. For RSSI, I calculate
the mean signal strength separately for each beacon within the same five-minute
window. This reduces sample-level radio noise and gives a relative indication
of which beacon has the strongest connection to the wearable. The room of that
beacon becomes the raw room estimate.

### 中文理解

首先把三轴加速度合成为模长：

\[
m(t)=\sqrt{a_x(t)^2+a_y(t)^2+a_z(t)^2}
\]

这样可以减少只观察单轴时受到手环方向的影响。在每个固定5分钟窗口内：

- magnitude mean表示模长的平均水平；
- magnitude SD表示模长围绕局部均值波动了多少；
- SD较小表示手环信号比较稳定；
- SD较大通常表示手腕运动、姿态变化或振动。

RSSI方面，对每个beacon分别计算5分钟平均RSSI。平均可以减少瞬时无线电噪声，然后选择平均
RSSI最高的beacon，并把它所在房间作为raw room estimate。

### Important caution / 重要限制

Low ACC variability is not identical to sleep. A quiet awake period can also
have low variability, and sensor noise can increase it. It is therefore a proxy
for signal stability, which must be combined with duration and continuity.

低ACC波动不能直接等同于睡眠。安静清醒也可能是低波动，传感器噪声也可能提高标准差。
所以它只是signal-stability proxy，还需要结合持续时间和连续性。

## Question 3: Why are these the correct things to plot?

## 问题3：为什么选择画这些变量？

### Suggested viva answer — English

The original mean-RSSI and ACC plot is useful as a diagnostic plot. It shows
which beacons were observed, their relative signal strengths, signal crossings,
RSSI gaps, and the broad alignment with movement. However, mean RSSI does not
directly show a room transition. The research question concerns changes in the
categorical strongest-beacon room. I therefore retained the mean-RSSI plot for
signal inspection, but changed the report-facing figures to directly compare
the raw and corrected categorical room timelines, with the main-sleep episode
and movement context aligned in time. This representation more directly shows
what the algorithm changes and why that change may be justified.

### 中文理解

原来的mean-RSSI与ACC图并不是错误，而是用途有限。它适合检查：

- 哪些beacon被观察到；
- 信号强弱及交叉；
- RSSI缺失；
- RSSI与ACC时间是否对齐。

但mean RSSI主要说明无线电信号强弱或相对接近程度，并不能直接表示房间转换。真正的研究问题
是strongest-beacon room label是否发生变化。因此报告主图改成：

- raw categorical room；
- corrected categorical room；
- main-sleep episode；
- 统一时间轴上的movement context。

这样可以直接回答“原始算法哪里不合理”和“修正算法具体改变了什么”。

### Defence distinction / 答辩时要区分

> Mean-RSSI plot = signal diagnostic evidence.

> Raw-versus-corrected room plot = algorithmic outcome evidence.

## Question 5: Why use ACC magnitude SD rather than mean magnitude as stillness?

## 问题5：为什么使用ACC模长标准差，而不是模长均值判断静止？

### Suggested viva answer — English

Mean magnitude would be a reasonable first approximation if the feature
represented gravity-removed dynamic acceleration. In this dataset, however, the
raw magnitude includes an approximately constant gravity-related baseline, so
its mean remains close to one in both quiet and active windows. The median mean
magnitudes were 1.0069 and 1.0118 for the EF-001 low- and higher-motion groups,
which are very similar. In contrast, the median magnitude standard deviations
were 0.00293 and 0.08380. Standard deviation therefore provides a clearer
description of within-window movement-related fluctuation for these data. I do
not claim that SD is universally optimal; a future comparison should include
gravity-removed features such as ENMO, MAD, or dynamic acceleration magnitude.

### 中文理解

导师提出mean magnitude是合理的第一近似，但关键在于是否去除了重力。

当前使用的模长包含近似恒定的重力基线，所以静止和活动窗口的mean magnitude都接近1：

| EF-001组别 | Magnitude mean中位数 | Magnitude SD中位数 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Low motion | 1.0069 | 0.00293 |
| Higher motion | 1.0118 | 0.08380 |

mean差异很小，而SD差异更明显，所以当前数据下SD更适合描述窗口内动态波动。

但这不意味着SD永远最优。未来应比较：

- gravity-removed dynamic acceleration；
- ENMO；
- MAD；
- activity counts；
- magnitude SD。

### Methodological honesty / 方法学诚实性

The low/higher-motion groups are themselves defined using SD, so this numerical
comparison is descriptive rather than an independent validation of the feature.

低/高运动组本身由SD阈值定义，因此这个数值比较只是描述性支持，不是对SD特征的独立验证。

## Question 6: What is the ACC log-space boundary and why use it?

## 问题6：ACC的log-space boundary是什么，为什么使用它？

### Suggested viva answer — English

ACC standard deviation is positive and strongly right-skewed, so relative
differences are more meaningful than equal absolute differences. I therefore
clustered log10 ACC SD. The decision boundary between two adjacent cluster
centres is their midpoint in log space. When transformed back to the original
scale, this is the geometric mean of the centres. For EF-001, the quiet and
transitional centres were 0.01379 and 0.03830, giving

\[
\sqrt{0.01379\times0.03830}=0.02298\approx0.023.
\]

The value 0.023 is therefore the equal-distance point between two fitted states
on the clustering scale, rather than a threshold chosen by eye. The four-state
model and its interpretation remain exploratory and dataset-specific.

### 中文理解

ACC SD为正数并且明显右偏。运动强度从0.003到0.006，更自然地理解为“翻倍”，而不是简单
增加0.003。因此先计算：

\[
z=\log_{10}(ACC\ SD)
\]

然后在log scale上聚类。相邻两个cluster centre在log scale上的中点，转换回原尺度就是几何
平均数：

\[
boundary=\sqrt{c_1c_2}
\]

EF-001：

\[
\sqrt{0.01379\times0.03830}=0.02298\approx0.023
\]

因此0.023不是肉眼选出的数字，而是quiet和transitional cluster之间的等距离决策点。

### Limitation / 限制

The number of clusters was selected as an exploratory representation of
near-still, quiet, transitional, and active windows. These are not supervised
activity labels, and the threshold should not yet be treated as universal.

四个cluster只是对near-still、quiet、transitional和active状态的探索性描述，不是有真实标签
监督训练的活动分类，也不能暂时视为通用阈值。

## Question 7: What does the sleep-duration log-space boundary mean?

## 问题7：睡眠episode时长的log-space boundary是什么意思？

### Suggested viva answer — English

Episode duration is also positive and right-skewed, so I cluster log duration
rather than raw minutes. For EF-001, the fitted short-rest and main-sleep
centres were approximately 111 and 559 minutes. Their log-space midpoint becomes
the geometric mean on the original scale:

\[
\sqrt{111\times559}\approx249\text{ minutes}.
\]

I would not interpret 249 minutes as a physiological definition of sleep. The
stronger evidence is that the longest short-rest candidate was about 195 minutes
and the shortest main-sleep candidate was 465 minutes, leaving a clear empty
interval. Any boundary inside that interval would produce the same episode
classification. The EF-002 boundary of approximately 257 minutes was derived
independently from its intermediate and main-sleep centres of 146 and 452
minutes.

### 中文理解

episode duration同样是正数和右偏数据，所以对log duration聚类。

EF-001中心为：

- short rest：111分钟；
- main sleep：559分钟。

几何平均分界为：

\[
\sqrt{111\times559}\approx249\text{分钟}
\]

但249分钟不是生理学睡眠定义。更重要的是：

- 最长short rest约195分钟；
- 最短main sleep约465分钟；
- 195到465分钟之间没有候选episode。

因此只要分界位于这个空白区间，分类都不会改变。EF-002的257分钟则由它自己的146分钟和
452分钟中心独立得到，没有直接复制EF-001。

## Question 8: Are you using fixed or sliding windows, and what are the trade-offs?

## 问题8：使用的是固定窗还是滑动窗？它们各有什么优缺点？

### Suggested viva answer — English

I use three temporal structures for different purposes. First, the base ACC and
RSSI features use fixed, non-overlapping five-minute windows. This gives one
aligned and auditable row per interval, but limits temporal resolution and can
split an event at a boundary. Second, the initial low-motion RSSI correction
uses a centred 30- or 60-minute sliding window updated every five minutes. It
suppresses isolated RSSI changes, but adjacent outputs share most observations,
the winning room can change as the window moves, and a centred window uses
future data, so it is an offline method. Third, the final EF-001 method uses a
variable-duration episode. This maintains one room hypothesis across a complete
sleep-like period, but depends on episode-boundary assumptions and cannot be
finalised until sufficient context is available.

### 中文理解

当前不是只使用一种窗口，而是三种结构服务于不同目的：

| 类型 | 用途 | 优点 | 缺点 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 固定、不重叠5分钟窗 | ACC与RSSI基础特征 | 时间对齐清楚、容易复现、每个样本只进入一个基础窗 | 事件可能被边界拆开，时间精度只有5分钟 |
| 居中30/60分钟滑窗，每5分钟更新 | 初始low-motion多数票 | 利用更多邻近证据，减少孤立RSSI跳变 | 相邻输出高度相关，窗口移动时winner仍可能变化，并使用未来数据 |
| Variable-duration episode | 最终EF-001睡眠修正 | 整段保持一致房间假设，符合睡眠场景 | 依赖episode边界与持续时间，需要观察完整上下文 |

如果未来需要实时部署，不能直接使用centered window。需要改成trailing window，或者等待
episode结束后再输出最终判断。

## Question 9: How was this approach refined compared with earlier phases?

## 问题9：与之前阶段相比，第七阶段的方法做了哪些改进？

### Suggested viva answer — English

The earlier Fourth and Fifth Phase method used movement to choose a longer RSSI
smoothing window. I retained that principle but did not copy the algorithm
unchanged. For EF-001 and EF-002, I derived participant-specific ACC thresholds
rather than reusing 0.010, detected low-motion candidates across the full day
rather than imposing an 18:00–10:00 night range, allowed a 15-minute movement
interruption so that turning did not split one sleep period, and clustered bout
durations to identify long sleep-like episodes. I first tested 30- and 60-minute
votes, found that moving-window membership still caused switching, and therefore
introduced an episode-dominant room for EF-001. For EF-002, where the main issue
was RSSI disappearance rather than switching, I used a different conservative
rule: fill a gap as Bedroom only if both 30-minute contexts support Bedroom.
Thus, the refinement was driven by the failure mode of each dataset rather than
by forcing one smoothing rule onto both.

### 中文理解

第四、第五阶段的核心思想是：

```text
低运动 → 使用更长RSSI窗口进行平滑
```

第七阶段保留了这个原则，但没有直接照搬算法，而是逐步改进：

1. 不再复用旧的 `0.010` ACC threshold；
2. EF-001和EF-002分别进行ACC clustering；
3. 不再预设 `18:00–10:00` 夜间范围；
4. 全天检测low-motion candidates；
5. 允许15分钟短暂运动，避免翻身把一次睡眠拆开；
6. 先比较30分钟与60分钟滑窗；
7. 发现滑窗winner仍会变化后，EF-001改为episode-dominant room；
8. RSSI缺失不会自动被平滑成房间；
9. EF-002只填补前后30分钟都支持Bedroom的缺失；
10. 不支持的700分钟gap继续保留missing。

方法发展路径是：

```text
single-window threshold
→ movement-adaptive sliding window
→ all-day low-motion episode detection
→ episode-level room correction
→ evidence-supported missing-data recovery
```

### Strong closing statement / 适合答辩结尾的表述

> The refinement was not simply stronger smoothing. It changed the temporal
> unit of inference from an individual window to a behaviourally coherent
> episode, while preserving missingness when spatial evidence was insufficient.

> 这个改进不只是“更强的平滑”，而是把推断单位从单个窗口提升为行为上连贯的episode，同时
> 在空间证据不足时继续保留missing和不确定性。

## General Limitations to State Proactively / 建议主动说明的总体限制

### English

1. There is no independent ground-truth room label, so the output is a
   movement-supported interpretation rather than validated location truth.
2. Low ACC variability is not specific to sleep.
3. The cluster counts, thresholds, and 15-minute interruption tolerance are
   exploratory and require external validation.
4. The centred sliding vote and complete-episode correction are offline methods.
5. Assigning one dominant room assumes that repeated room changes are unlikely
   during a long low-motion episode; this is plausible but not directly proven.
6. The current evidence comes from a small number of participants and homes.

### 中文

1. 没有独立真实房间标签，因此结果是movement-supported interpretation，不是经过验证的
   location ground truth。
2. 低ACC波动并不是睡眠特异性指标。
3. cluster数量、阈值和15分钟interruption tolerance仍属于探索性设计，需要外部验证。
4. centered sliding vote和完整episode correction目前属于离线方法。
5. 整段使用dominant room依赖一个假设：长low-motion episode内不太可能反复换房间。这个
   假设合理，但没有被真实房间标签直接证明。
6. 当前参与者和住宅数量有限。

## Final 30-Second Summary / 最后30秒总结

### English

My method separates spatial inference from movement plausibility. Five-minute
mean RSSI provides a raw room proposal, while five-minute ACC magnitude
variability identifies low-motion context. Data-derived duration clustering then
groups sustained low motion into sleep-like episodes. In EF-001, implausible
within-episode RSSI switching is replaced by the episode-dominant observed room.
In EF-002, only RSSI gaps with two-sided Bedroom support are filled. The method
therefore reduces implausible room changes while explicitly retaining unsupported
missing data and uncertainty.

### 中文

我的方法把空间推断与运动合理性判断分开：5分钟平均RSSI提供原始房间候选，5分钟ACC模长
波动提供低运动背景，数据驱动的时长聚类再把持续低运动组合为睡眠样episode。EF-001使用
episode dominant room修正睡眠内不合理切换；EF-002只填补前后都支持Bedroom的RSSI缺失。
因此，该方法在减少不合理房间变化的同时，仍然明确保留证据不足的missing和不确定性。
